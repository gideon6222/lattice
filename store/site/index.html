About The Lattice

The Lattice is a mining game with a lock on it. Anchors are buried across a planet, three under each of three barriers that nothing in your drill ship can touch. Break a barrier's three Anchors and its core lights up. Break the core and the way down opens. The last core is the door of the Vault at the center, and that is the end.

Hold down and the ship digs. Everything below you is rock until you cut it, and everything you cut is a shaft you have to climb back up. The planet is 61 columns wide and 452 metres deep, in twelve regions that each have their own rock and their own rules.

Dig where you like

  • There is no route: the whole face is diggable, and the shaft is whatever shape you make it
  • Cutting straight is fast and cutting wide finds more
  • Marks at the top of the screen fill as you break a depth's Anchors

Keys you never sell

Copper, iron, silver and gold are money, sold at the pad. Deeper down are the keys: amethyst, emerald, ruby and rarer things, one crystal at a time in small pockets. Keys are never sold. They are what the better parts of the ship are made from, so every find is a moment and every upgrade is a hunt.

The planet comes apart

Break the first core and the ground stops staying where you left it. Every Anchor you break after that shows in the rock, then in the air, and you can patch the scars they leave by packing ore into them.

Twelve wrecks

Twelve wrecked drill ships are buried across the planet, one in every region. You see the shape of one in the dark before you know what it is, its own lamp still faintly lit. Cut through and the hold still holds what the crew had dug.

Fuel is the clock

Nothing hunts you, but the ground is not safe. Gas pockets open the hull, deep rock cooks it, and tremors close tunnels behind you. Every metre costs the same going down as coming up, so turning around with half a tank left is the correct play.
