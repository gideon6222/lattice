# Found and earned, The Lattice's 0.57.0 major update (Play promotional content)

Type: Major update.

Dates: set by the coordinator at submission.

Event name (for us only):
The Lattice 0.57.0: Found and earned

Tagline:
Earn new gifts for your drill ship and see them on the hull.

Description:
The Lattice 0.57.0 is about what you find and what you earn. The fitting bay now shows only what you own, with a Next line to guide you. A new Ledger lists your feats. Sell a full hold for the Ore Sorter, ride out gas for the Pressure Seal, cut hard rock for the Resonance Tip, and lose a ship to earn the Return Beacon. Crates hide a Flare Line and an Arc Lance. Every gift shows on your hull. Menus are cleaner.

Pictures: event.png (1920x1080), a real frame of the game with the HUD hidden and no words: the
drill ship in a side tunnel lit end to end by its Flare Line, a gold-rich wall above and below it,
with the earned gifts fitted and showing on the hull (sorter drum, seal, tip, beacon, lance). Made
with `studio run event-art` (tools/event-art.mjs, the landscape twin of tools/store.mjs): the built
game drawn at 3840x2160 in headless Chrome and scaled down to 1920x1080 RGB with no alpha.
